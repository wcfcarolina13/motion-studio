scripts/stack.py and scripts/stack.js adapt the sprite-stacking method from https://github.com/danielwh2/sprite-stacking
(commit 6fecdfd, skills/sprite-stacking/scripts/views_to_slices.py and SKILL.md). They were rewritten for this engine,
but a few parts of stack.py follow the original closely (the edge-distance scans in carve, the 2 x 2 block test in
thin_layers), so those portions are used under the MIT License below.
