# House notes — read by motion-studio before every film

Copy to the path in `~/.motion-studio.json` → "house" and make it yours. Everything here is optional.

## Style
- Palette: ground #0b0b0b, ink #e8e6e1, grey #969691, accent #e8772e (the kit's defaults). Georgia for type.
- A publication's or product's own brand wins over the house look.
- Series rules, e.g.: one idea per video; a blunt one-line punchline; end on an honest handoff.
- Default format: 4:5.

## Standing rules
- Recorded or licensed audio only.
- Nothing is posted by the agent.
- Topics to check before they appear in a film (employer, clients, private material): …
- Product truthfulness rules (prices, reviews, claims): …

## Reviewers
- Blind readers: a fresh Claude subagent, plus optionally a second model via the Codex CLI (`codex exec`).
- Panel: which model plays the motion-design reviewer, the creative-director reviewer and the advisor.
